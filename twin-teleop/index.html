FR3 Duo Digital Twin for Tabletop Teleoperation

Gaussian Splatting 6-DoF Object Tracking VR Teleoperation three.js

An operator wearing a VR headset teleoperates a dual-arm Franka FR3 station from inside a live digital twin of its table. The scene is a Gaussian-splat scan of the station, the arms follow the robot's joint state, and every object on the table is a scanned mesh whose pose is tracked in real time. Below are four recorded episodes, each replayed in the twin twice: once from the operator's own head pose, with the station's camera in the corner for comparison, and once from a camera circling the table.
