FR3 Duo Digital Twin for Tabletop Teleoperation.

Gaussian Splatting · 6-DoF Object Tracking · VR Teleoperation · three.js

An operator wearing a VR headset teleoperates a dual-arm Franka FR3 station from inside a live digital twin of its table. The scene is a Gaussian-splat scan of the station, the arms follow the robot's joint state, and every object on the table is a scanned mesh whose pose is tracked in real time.
